Jan 2024 - Dec 2026 (in progress)

  Fatec is São Paulo's network of state-run technology colleges: public,
  tuition-free, and entered by a competitive entrance exam.

  A three-year undergraduate technology degree, aimed at building software
  rather than at research.

  The entrance exam is the one ~/projects/estudafatec was built for. I sat
  it before I wrote the platform for it.
