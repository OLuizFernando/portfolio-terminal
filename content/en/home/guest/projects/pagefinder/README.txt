PageFinder

A reading tracker. Books move between a reading list, a wish list and a
finished list, carrying the last page read and how far in you are.

My final project for Harvard's CS50x. The catalogue is community-driven: if a
book or an author is missing, whoever hits the gap fills it in, and everyone
else gets it. Flask, Postgres, and the first schema I designed on paper before
writing any of it.
